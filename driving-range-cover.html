Designing a driving range cover

Three designs, 30+ material options and a 20-page report, so a golf course could keep its range open in bad weather.

Role
Research & Design Engineer
Client
Shuswap Lake Golf Course, Blind Bay BC
When
Apr 2024 to Sep 2025
Tools
SolidWorks, cost analysis, technical report

    done
    planned

    Define

    Keep the range open through rain and snow

    The golf course wanted a cover over its 90 × 18 ft driving range so members could keep practicing through rain and snow.

    My job was to figure out what that cover should be: the design, the materials, how long it would take, what it would cost, and when in the year to build it without getting in golfers' way.

    Research

    30+ material options, 26 sources

    I compared more than 30 material and bill-of-materials options across the designs. Everything that went into the final report is backed by 26 sources.

    Brainstorm

    Three designs in SolidWorks

    I came up with three different structures and went through 5+ iterations on each. Pick one to see how it compares.

    Select

    The metal roof, with a budget option beside it

    I recommended the metal roof. It costs the most, but it lasts 50 to 70 years and handles BC snow and wind the best.

    For a tighter budget, I suggested the portable covers, since they're cheap, quick to set up, and could be rented out to golfers on bad-weather days.
