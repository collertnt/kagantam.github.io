03internship
Research & Design Engineer · Shuswap Lake Golf Course, Blind Bay BC · Apr 2024 – Sep 2025

Designing a driving range cover

The golf course wanted a cover over its 90 × 18 ft driving range so members could keep practicing through rain and snow. My job was to figure out what that cover should be: the design, the materials, how long it would take, what it would cost, and when in the year to build it without getting in golfers' way.

I came up with three designs in SolidWorks, went through 5+ iterations on each, and compared 30+ material and bill-of-materials options. It all went into a 20-page report with 26 sources. I presented it to the general manager in September 2025, then updated the final report with his feedback.

DesignCost (CAD)LifespanWeatherBuild time
Tensile structure$8,7865–10 yrsDecent4–7 days
Metal roof$33,85450–70 yrsStrong~14 weeks
Portable cover$2,828 / unit3–5 yrsLow1–2 days

I recommended the metal roof. It costs the most, but it lasts 50 to 70 years and handles BC snow and wind the best. For a tighter budget, I suggested the portable covers, since they're cheap, quick to set up, and could be rented out to golfers on bad-weather days.

04volleyball

From the bench to MVP

When I made my club team, it was ranked in the top 20 in Canada, and I barely played. Most matches I watched from the bench.

So I tried to learn as much as I could. I reached out to specific coaches and asked them to teach me. At my games, I'd ask coaches to watch just a point or two and give me a quick pointer afterward. I watched a lot of game film, of good players and of myself.

It took a while, but practice turned into minutes, and minutes turned into a starting spot. In 2025 I was named MVP and got the Oakville Thunder Coaches' Choice Award.

In 2026 I captained Iroquois Ridge to the Halton Sr. Boys Tier 1 championship and was named the team's 2025–26 MVP. I also went to Volleyball Canada Nationals in Calgary with my club team.

What it taught me

Sitting on the bench taught me to stay ready and keep working when nobody's watching. Being captain taught me to look out for the guys who were where I used to be. A team is only as good as how much the players trust each other, and that's true off the court too.
