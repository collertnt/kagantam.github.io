    Role

    Materials Research and Design Engineer

    Formula Nano is a design team at the University of Waterloo. I joined in September 2026, at the start of first year. My work has two halves: mechanisms and simulations.

    Research

    Checking compounds in simulation before the lab

    I run quantum chemistry simulations with ORCA and Density Functional Theory (DFT) to evaluate molecular compatibility, binding affinities and reaction energetics between candidate compounds.

    Modelling reaction pathways and electronic structures predicts whether a synthesis is likely to work. That guides which materials the team picks and speeds up experimental prototyping for nanoscale fabrication.

    Develop

    Mechanisms for a custom probing tool

    I develop and refine mechanisms for a custom probing tool. The aim is to streamline microfabrication testing procedures, so that characterizing a device is more accessible and more computationally efficient.
